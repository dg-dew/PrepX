AI Interviewer
Walk me through a project where you had to make an unpopular technical decision. How did you get the team aligned?
You · 42s
In our capstone we were building a payments layer. The team wanted to ship on Firebase because it was fast. I pushed back and proposed Postgres with a Prisma layer — slower to set up, but auditable. I built a 2-day spike to prove migrations wouldn't block us, presented it with cost + audit tradeoffs, and the team agreed. We shipped on time and had zero data incidents in 8 weeks.
AI Interviewer
Good — tell me one thing you'd change about how you led that alignment.
You · 28s
I would have surfaced the cost model on day one instead of day three. I lost credibility for two days because the team assumed I was choosing complexity for its own sake.
